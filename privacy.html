Privacy notice

Who is responsible

Joseph Berkner, Elsewhere Maps, Neuplachter Weg 5, 17268 Templin, Germany. E-mail: kontakt@elsewhere-maps.com.

What is stored and why

When you send the form, your Etsy order number and your choices for the map (centre, title, language, units, cities and special places) are stored so that I can design and print your map (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, performance of a contract). Your IP address is used only for the security check and to block abuse and is not stored with your design. Your data is used only for your Etsy order: no newsletter, no advertising, no sharing with anyone except the print partner, who receives only the finished print file.

Services used

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website (elsewhere-maps.com) and the personal design page for your order and receives the usual connection data when you open them. Neither site uses cookies, tracking or analytics.
  • Scaleway (Scaleway SAS, France, data centre in Paris) receives the form, checks it and passes it on. The security check against automated abuse (ALTCHA) runs in your browser and on this server, without cookies and without any third party.
  • GitHub stores your design as an entry in a private repository that only I can access.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK) delivers the map tiles and the place search. Your browser connects to its servers when you use the maps.

Scaleway processes your data within the EU (Paris). GitHub, Inc. is based in the USA; the transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR), under which GitHub is certified, and on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. For the United Kingdom (OpenStreetMap Foundation) an adequacy decision of the European Commission applies.

How long

Your design is deleted 3 months after your order has been delivered, unless the law requires me to keep it longer.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your data and object to its processing. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
