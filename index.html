About me

Hi 👋 I’m Joseph, and this is my small map studio.

I grew up in rural Germany, among quiet landscapes and long roads, with plenty of space to look and think. Early on I fell for data, patterns and maps, and for the stories they quietly tell about the world. That curiosity led me to study geography, geoinformatics, computer science and data analysis.

Today I combine precise analysis with careful design and turn raw data into pieces that are both accurate and meaningful. After some years away I moved back to my hometown, where life has slowed down again in the best way. Living here with my twins has taught me to make things with intention, care and lasting quality.

I don’t want to make standard products. Every map should feel personal and a little unexpected, with a story hidden in the data.

My test is simple: a piece is finished when I would happily hang it in my own home.
